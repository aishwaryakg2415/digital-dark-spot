Websites copy each other. We catch it, so repeated text can’t pass for agreement.

This is the part no search engine, summariser or chatbot does. Ten copies of one article look like ten confirmations. Digital Dark Spot compares every pair of sources, finds copied passages, and counts independent sources, not pages. In our demo, five pages turn out to be only four independent sources.
