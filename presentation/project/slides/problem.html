The problem

Your research looks finished. It isn’t.

You ask “Is it bad to charge my phone overnight?” and read five pages:

Looks fine

Three say overnight charging is safe.

Outdated

A forum answer from 2013 says it can make the battery catch fire.

Conflict

One says drain to 0% every month. The maker says don’t.

Copied

Two of the “safe” pages are the same article, copied.

Missing

None says what to do when the battery wears out.

Digital Dark Spot

2

Here’s an everyday example. Five pages, and every one of the problems the hackathon brief describes is hiding in them: an outdated page, a contradiction, a missing answer. Plus one more that people rarely notice: two of the pages that agree are actually the same article copied. Reading them, you’d feel you’d done your research. You haven’t.
