The solution

One question in. Five kinds of dark spots out.

Gaps

Things a complete answer needs that no source covers

Conflicts

Sources that disagree, quoted side by side

Echoes

Pages that copied each other, counted as one

Freshness

Old dates, past cycles and expired deadlines

Checklist

What to verify next, ranked P1 to P3

Plus a readiness score from 0 to 100, and every finding opens the source with the exact passage highlighted.

Digital Dark Spot

4

You type any question and add your sources, as URLs or pasted text. Digital Dark Spot returns five kinds of findings: gaps, conflicts, echoes, freshness warnings, and a prioritised checklist of what to verify next. And a readiness score so you know at a glance how far you can trust what you’ve found.
