Six steps. The AI does what it’s good at: understanding the question and reading pages. Every AI output is then checked by code. Quotes are verified against the page, and dates, numbers and copying are all handled by rules, because that’s exactly where language models slip. For any topic the AI drafts the checklist of what a complete answer needs; for known decisions like scholarships we use built-in expert templates.
